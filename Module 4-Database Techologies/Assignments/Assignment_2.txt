**************************************Queries on Select command with Where and Date function.***************************************


Q.1:-Display the Employee Name, Job, and Start Date of Employees hired from February 1981 to May 1981.

Query:-select ename 'Employee Name',Job,hiredate as 'Start Date' from emp where hiredate BETWEEN '1981-02-01' AND '1981-05-31';

Output:-

+---------------+----------+------------+
| Employee Name | Job      | Start Date |
+---------------+----------+------------+
| ALLEN         | SALESMAN | 1981-02-20 |
| WARD          | SALESMAN | 1981-02-22 |
| JONES         | MANAGER  | 1981-04-02 |
| BLAKE         | MANAGER  | 1981-05-01 |
+---------------+----------+------------+
4 rows in set (0.00 sec)

Q.2:-Display the Name and Hiredate of every Employee who was hired in 1982.

Query:- select Ename as Name,hiredate from emp where year(hiredate)=1982;
